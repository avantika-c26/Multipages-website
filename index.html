Our Facilities

Smart Classes

smart class is a technologically enhanced classroom that replaces traditional chalkboards with digital tools like interactive flat panels, projectors, and multimedia content. It transforms learning by bringing abstract concepts to life through animations, videos, and real-time digital collaboration, making education highly engaging and accessible.

Library

school library is a centralized learning hub that provides students and staff with equitable access to print and digital resources, fostering literacy, critical thinking, and a lifelong love of reading.

Sports

School sports foster physical fitness, teamwork, and discipline through competitive leagues and recreational programs. Programs typically span a wide range of activities, including football, cricket, basketball, swimming, athletics, and badminton.
